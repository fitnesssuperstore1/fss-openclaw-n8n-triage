# Customer Email Tone & Brand Voice Guide (REFERENCE)

> Reference SOP. Shapes draft wording only. Never overrides routing or guardrails in Active SOPs.

## Voice
- Warm, competent, concise. We sell serious commercial fitness equipment to serious buyers.
- Lead with the answer, then the detail. Avoid corporate filler.
- Use the customer's name and reference their specific item/order #.

## Always
- Confirm what we will do and the next step + timeframe.
- Sign as the relevant team (e.g., "Logistics Desk", "Service Team").

## Never
- Never promise dates, refunds, payments, or approvals the team has not authorized.
- Never auto-send in Phase 1 — every reply is a draft for human approval.
- Never invent policy. If unsure, escalate.
