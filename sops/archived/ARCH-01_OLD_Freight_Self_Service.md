# [ARCHIVED] Freight Tracking — Customer Self-Service

> ⚠️ ARCHIVED. This SOP MUST NOT control routing or customer guidance.
> Superseded by **SOP-02 (Active)**. Retained for historical reference only.

## Former trigger
Customer asks about freight delivery scheduling or tracking after pickup.

## Former (OUTDATED) policy
1. Provide the customer the PRO number and the freight carrier's phone number.
2. **Tell the customer to contact the carrier directly to schedule their own delivery appointment.**
3. Fitness Superstore does **not** coordinate freight delivery scheduling.

## Why archived
This self-service approach caused missed deliveries, redelivery fees, and poor CX. As of 2026, the **Logistics Desk coordinates delivery on the customer's behalf** — see **SOP-02 (Active)**.

## Conflict handling
If triage matches this archived SOP for a freight question, IGNORE it for the decision, follow SOP-02, and raise `conflict_flag` noting "ARCH-01 archived, superseded by SOP-02".
