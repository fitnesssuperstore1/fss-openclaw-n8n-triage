# Post-Delivery Damage & Refund Handling

## Trigger
Customer reports product arrived damaged and/or demands a refund, often emotionally / urgently.

## Routing
Primary lane: **Install / Service / Warranty** (damage resolution). The **refund decision** is a financial action — flag `approval_required: true`, approver **CS Lead** (refunds) / **Owner** if amount exceeds threshold.

## Handling
1. Acknowledge fast and empathetically. Do NOT promise a refund or dollar amount in the draft — AI cannot approve refunds.
2. Request photos of damage + packaging and the order #, within the freight-damage claim window.
3. Offer the resolution path: replacement parts, replacement unit, service visit, or refund — **to be confirmed by a human** after review.
4. If freight-related, open a freight damage claim (links to SOP-02 carrier).

## Guardrails
- AI draft = empathetic acknowledgement + info request + "a specialist will confirm the resolution." No refund authorization.
- Escalate immediately if the customer threatens chargeback/legal.

## Draft tone
Empathetic, urgent, ownership-taking, but **no financial commitment**. Draft-only.
