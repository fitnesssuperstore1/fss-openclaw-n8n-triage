# Parcel / Small-Package Claims

## Trigger
Small-parcel carrier (UPS / FedEx / USPS) shows **delivered**, but the customer says they **did not receive** it. Accessories, small parts, supplements — NOT freight/LTL equipment.

## Routing
Primary lane: **Parcel / Small Package**. This is distinct from **Shipping CS (freight)** — never route a parcel claim to the freight lane or vice versa.

## Handling
1. Pull the parcel tracking + delivery scan details (date/time, geo, photo if available).
2. Ask the customer to check common locations, with neighbors, and confirm shipping address.
3. If still missing, open a **parcel carrier lost/stolen claim** and follow the carrier's claim window/process.
4. Offer reship/refund **per claim outcome** — note refund decisions follow SOP-04 approval rules.

## Guardrails
- Distinguish parcel vs freight explicitly in the routing note.
- Do not promise claim approval; carriers decide.

## Draft tone
Helpful, methodical, sets claim expectations. Draft-only.
