# Financing & Pre-Purchase Sales Inquiries

## Trigger
Prospective customer asks about financing, payment plans, or pricing **before** placing an order. No order exists in Shopify yet.

## Routing
Primary lane: **Sales CS**. This is a pre-sale inquiry, not Finance/ACH (that lane is for vendor payments / ACH approvals, not consumer financing).

## Handling
1. Share the approved consumer financing options and the application link (e.g., third-party financing partner). Use only published terms.
2. Do NOT quote custom rates, approval odds, or guarantees — those are set by the financing provider.
3. Offer to help configure the right SKU/quote and connect them to a sales rep.
4. Capture the equipment of interest for sales follow-up.

## Guardrails
- No promises of approval or specific APR.
- Consumer financing ≠ vendor ACH. Do not route to Finance/Owner.

## Draft tone
Helpful, sales-forward, includes financing link. Draft-only.
