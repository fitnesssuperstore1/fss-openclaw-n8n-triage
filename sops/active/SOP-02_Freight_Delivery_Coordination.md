# Freight Delivery Coordination & Tracking (Post-Pickup)

## Trigger
Freight / LTL shipment **already picked up and tracking exists**. Customer needs delivery scheduling, missed the carrier's call, or asks for status. Large equipment delivered by LTL freight (not parcel).

## Routing
Primary lane: **Shipping CS**. Freight only — do NOT route parcel (UPS/FedEx small package) here; use SOP-07.

## Handling (CURRENT POLICY)
1. Look up the PRO / freight tracking number and current carrier status.
2. **Our Logistics Desk coordinates delivery scheduling on the customer's behalf.** We contact the freight carrier and arrange the appointment window. We do NOT abandon the customer to the carrier.
3. If the customer missed the carrier's call, collect 2–3 preferred delivery windows and a callback number; Logistics re-initiates scheduling.
4. Confirm liftgate / threshold / inside-delivery needs were on the order.

## Note
This SOP **supersedes ARCH-01**, which told customers to self-schedule directly with the carrier. ARCH-01 is archived and must not control routing.
