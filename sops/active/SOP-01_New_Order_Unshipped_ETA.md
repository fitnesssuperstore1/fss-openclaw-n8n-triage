# New Order / Pending Shipment ETA

## Trigger
Customer asks when a **paid but unfulfilled** order will ship, and **no tracking exists yet** (Shopify status: paid / unfulfilled). Commonly large commercial equipment (e.g., functional trainers, racks).

## Routing
Primary lane: **New Orders / Pending Shipments**. This is NOT a Shipping CS case yet — there is no tracking to act on.

## Handling
1. Confirm order is located and paid in Shopify (order #, item, order date).
2. Provide the published lead time for the SKU/category. Commercial freight items: typical build/fulfillment window 2–4 weeks before a carrier is assigned.
3. Set expectation: customer will receive tracking + a freight delivery-scheduling call once the carrier picks up (hand-off to SOP-02 at that point).
4. Do NOT invent a specific ship date. If lead time is unknown for the SKU, escalate to Order Management.

## Draft tone
Reassuring, specific to their item, no firm date unless published. Always draft-only — awaiting human approval before send.
