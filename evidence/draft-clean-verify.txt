# Fix #2 — no internal metadata in customer-visible drafts

## What was wrong
skills/draft_response/SKILL.md REQUIRED the draft body to end with an internal
footer:  [DRAFT — pending human approval | lane: <lane> | SOP: <sop_id> (Active)]
That leaked lane names + SOP ids into the customer-visible Gmail draft (and it
contradicted the skill's own "never quote SOP ids in the body" rule).

## Fix
Removed the footer requirement; draft_body must now be a clean customer-facing
reply only. Lane/SOP live only in the Monday card + audit fields. The approval-
pending state is tracked by the workflow + Monday "Awaiting Review", not by text
inside the draft.

## Verification (draft_response run directly, clear SOP-01 input)
draft_body produced:
---
Hi,

Thanks for reaching out about order FS-1234. Your paid order is in our
fulfillment queue, and tracking information will be sent once the treadmill
ships.

Best,
Fitness Superstore
---
Scan for internal metadata ([DRAFT / lane: / SOP- / REF- / ARCH- / pending
human approval): NONE — clean.
